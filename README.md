# 🌺 A Field of Remembrance | 1,000 Poppies

### A Community United in Remembrance

Welcome to **A Field of Remembrance**, a school-wide Remembrance Day art installation by **Enquiring Minds Montessori & Toronto Children's School of the Arts**.

Through the creation and arrangement of **1,000 red poppies**, students from Toddler through Grade 8 explore remembrance, gratitude, peace, and the power of working together.

Inspired by *In Flanders Fields*, the installation transforms the school lawn into a shared expression of remembrance while connecting artistic creation with hands-on learning.

---

## 🌺 Explore the Installation

**1,000 Poppies, One Community**

Each student helps prepare poppies from reflective red metallic paper. Together, the individual poppies form a striking field that changes with the natural light.

**🔢 Mathematics Made Visible**

Students explore the decimal system through groups of 10, 100, and 1,000. An interactive demonstration on the website shows how 1,000 poppies can be arranged in different rows and columns.

**📐 Geometry & Measurement**

Students investigate straight lines, spacing, grids, patterns, and measurement while planning the arrangement of the field.

**🎨 Visual Arts & Practical Life**

Cutting, painting, assembling, and installing the poppies help students develop coordination, concentration, independence, and artistic awareness.

**🕊️ History & Peace Education**

The installation introduces the meaning of the remembrance poppy and encourages age-appropriate reflection on gratitude, service, and peace.

---

## 🌱 Our Educational Philosophy

At the heart of **A Field of Remembrance** is the belief that meaningful learning happens through purposeful, hands-on experiences.

Students are encouraged to:

- Discover the meaning of remembrance through respectful participation.
- Experience large quantities through concrete mathematics.
- Explore geometry, measurement, repetition, and visual patterns.
- Develop practical life skills through careful, independent work.
- Recognize how individual contributions create something greater together.
- Reflect on kindness, gratitude, and peace.

Each poppy represents a student's contribution to a collective school community project.
